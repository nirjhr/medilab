👥 For Athletes, Coaches & Support Staff — Do's & Don'ts
🏃‍♂️⚡

Athlete

strict liability · your body, your responsibility

✅ ATHLETE DO's

  • Know WADA Prohibited List & update annually.
  • Check every medication/ supplement via Global DRO.
  • Apply for TUE before using banned substance (if medical need).
  • Cooperate with sample collection anytime.
  • Use only batch-tested supplements (Informed Sport).
‼️ STRICT LIABILITY

❌ DON'Ts

  • Never assume "natural" is safe.
  • Don't share or accept unknown products.
  • Don't refuse/miss doping tests.
  • Never tamper with sample or whereabouts.
📋🧠

Coach

mentor · role model · educator

✅ COACH DO's

  • Teach anti-doping rules & values to athletes.
  • Verify all team medications/supplements.
  • Promote food-first nutrition over risky supplements.
  • Complete WADA ADEL for coaches.
  • Report suspicious doping behavior to NADO.

❌ DON'Ts

  • Don't force “win at all costs” culture.
  • Never administer/possess prohibited substances.
  • Don't associate with banned personnel.
  • No covering up violations.
7/11 ADRVs apply to support staff
🩺🍎

Support Staff

doctors, physios, dietitians

✅ SUPPORT DO's

  • Prescribe only non-prohibited alternatives.
  • Assist athletes with TUE applications.
  • Stay updated on Prohibited List (Jan 1).
  • Promote supplement risk awareness.
  • Honest communication with anti-doping orgs.

❌ DON'Ts

  • No banned substance prescription without TUE.
  • Don't recommend unverified supplements.
  • Never traffic or possess prohibited items.
  • Don't ignore contamination risks in supplements.

🔔 NADO: Medical professionals can face bans too!

⚠️ Banned Supplements & Hidden Threats (WADA/NADA alert)
⚡🧪

Pre-workouts

high contamination risk

🚫 Hidden Stimulants

Flmodafinil, Fladrafinil, DMAA, Amphetamine analogs often found.

PERMANENTLY BANNED

⚠️ Even "legal high" labels may contain WADA S6 stimulants → positive test. Avoid unless batch-certified.

✅ Safer alternative: Caffeine within limits (urine <12μg/mL).

🔥💊

Fat Burners & SARMs

"test boosters" danger

❌ Banned Anabolics

SARMs (Ostarine, RAD140), BAM15, Aromatase inhibitors.

S1 / S4 PROHIBITED

These damage health + long bans up to 4 years. Many "natural" boosters contaminated.

🏥 NADA advisory: zero supplement likely 100% risk-free.

🥤🧬

Protein/BCAAs

cross-contamination

⚠️ Steroid Cross-Contamination

Anabolic steroids or clenbuterol traces found in some powders.

✅ Only use brands with "Informed Sport" / "Kölner Liste" seal.

📌 WADA: manufacturing defaults can cause inadvertent positives.

🏅 Sport‑Specific Regulations — Banned methods / substances by discipline
🚴‍♂️🏃‍♀️

Endurance Sports

Cycling · Marathon · Triathlon

🚫 Banned: EPO, Blood Doping, CO rebreathing

▪️ EPO & EPO mimetics (Pegmolesatide) → permanently banned.
▪️ Carbon monoxide inhalation (non-diagnostic) → prohibited method.
▪️ Blood transfusion or withdrawal/reinfusion → 4-year ban.
▪️ Salmeterol > allowed dose (100μg/8h) → violation.

WADA PROHIBITED LIST S2, M1, M2

💡 Always check your asthma meds: TUE needed for high-dose beta-2 agonists.

🏋️‍♂️💪

Strength & Power

Weightlifting · Sprint · Wrestling

⚡ Anabolic Agents & SARMs

▪️ All anabolic androgenic steroids (AAS) → S1 Ban.
▪️ SARMs (Ostarine, Ligandrol, RAD140) → severe bans.
▪️ BAM15 (mitochondrial uncoupler) classified as metabolic modulator.
▪️ Gene doping / myostatin inhibitors prohibited.

🔹 NADA India: most supplement contamination from this category.

PERMANENT PROHIBITION
⚽🏀🏏

Team / Ball Sports

Football · Cricket · Basketball

🎯 Stimulants & Glucocorticoids

▪️ Stimulants (Flmodafinil, cocaine, amphetamine) → in‑competition only ban.
▪️ Glucocorticoids: systemic injections or sustained-release Depo forms forbidden in competition.
▪️ Diuretics (masking agents) banned at all times.
▪️ Marijuana (cannabinoids) – has thresholds, but competition prohibited.

❗ Match day: beware of cold meds containing pseudoephedrine ( over limit ).

🎯🏌️‍♀️

Precision Sports

Archery · Shooting · Golf · Darts

🧘 Beta-Blockers Prohibited in Competition

▪️ Beta-blockers (Propranolol, Metoprolol, Atenolol) → S9 class: banned in-competition for precision sports.
▪️ Alcohol may be prohibited by some federations (WADA monitoring program).
▪️ Flmodafinil (stimulant) also banned for focus enhancement in competition.

WADA S9 / S6

✅ No beta-blockers on match day unless valid TUE for cardiovascular condition.

🥋🥊

Combat Sports

Boxing · Judo · MMA · Wrestling

⚖️ Diuretics & Rapid Weight Loss Ban

▪️ Diuretics & masking agents (S5) → banned all times, especially pre‑weigh‑in.
▪️ Anabolic agents for strength advantage → severe sanctions.
▪️ Stimulants (amphetamine type) increase aggression and pain tolerance → competition ban.
▪️ Glycerol (plasma expander) prohibited.

📢 NADA: weight manipulation using diuretics leads to health risks + 4-year suspension.

⛷️❄️

Winter Sports

Alpine Ski · Biathlon · Snowboard

🥶 Blood Doping & Hormones

▪️ EPO, blood manipulation, and altitude simulation via CO rebreathing banned.
▪️ Glucocorticoids: injectable forms restricted in competition.
▪️ Beta‑2 agonists (formoterol) high dose needs TUE.
▪️ Alcohol is prohibited in biathlon during competition (federation rule).

WADA PROHIBITED LIST ALWAYS UPDATED

📌 Therapeutic Use Exemption (TUE)

If you need a prohibited substance for a valid medical condition, apply for a TUE before competing. NADA / International Federation must approve.

📍 Whereabouts & Testing

Registered Testing Pool athletes: update 60-min time slot daily. 3 missed tests/filing failures in 12 months = violation.
